unpack v0.5.3 — staged extraction with archive checks

Unpack 0.5.3 hardens archive inspection and publishes extracted trees only after staging and audit.

  • Refuses dangerous ZIP and TAR member types and validates ZIP central and local records.
  • Runs listing and extraction against a private archive copy.
  • Extracts into private staging and publishes the destination atomically after the tree audit passes. Refusals report cleanup state.
  • The extraction budget remains a monitored soft limit, not a hard write-time cap.
